## Plan data (`data/`)

Generated by the scripts in `pipeline/` from files that the U.S. Office of Personnel Management (OPM) publishes for
the public: the FEHB and FEDVIP public use files (plan key, rates, benefits, service areas, dental and vision plans)
and the 2027 FEHB plan brochures. OPM's data files are U.S. government works. The plan brochures are written by the
insurance carriers and published by OPM; `data/brochure_facts.json` holds facts and short quotations taken from them
(each with its page number) so the app can cite its sources. The brochures themselves are not included. A plan's
brochure is the only contractual statement of its coverage.

This project is not affiliated with OPM or the U.S. government.
